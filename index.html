HOW IT WORKS

Three steps. Nothing uploaded.

01

Upload

Choose a PNG, JPG or WebP from your device.

02

Recolor

Pick a source color and replace it with any color you want.

03

Download

Export the edited image instantly as a PNG.

◎

Your images stay yours.

Recolor works entirely inside your browser using the Canvas API. Images are processed locally and are never sent to a server.
